Vol. 04 · Speaker

Kaelig Deloumeau-Prigent

Kaelig is a design engineering leader with fifteen years of design systems at Shopify, Salesforce, Intuit, and the Financial Times, and co-chairs the W3C Design Tokens Community Group. He’ll show how Claude Design, Claude Code, Storybook, and Playwright combine into one agentic workflow that revamps a product’s UI from head to toe, design system included, using the OpenID Foundation’s Conformance Test Suite redesign as the worked example.
