The lineup

Then we open it up. Madison Joslyn moderates a panel with David Zager and all three presenters, going deep on craft in the new landscape. Audience questions welcome.

Vol. 02 recap · Aug 6, 2026

Three talks, one messy middle.

Vol. 02 filled Pioneer Square Labs. Three people showed the actual work instead of talking about AI in the abstract.

Patrick Neeman dug into journey mapping and intent across the ecosystem of companies and products we use on a daily basis. His prototype does the heavy lifting and will empower product teams to agree on intent before anyone renders real UI.

Sarra Naemi took Stemtopia from design through engineering into a playable game and made agentic workflows look survivable. She then busted out the Xbox controllers and challenged the audience to a fighting game.

Ben Mullins pulled apart the 808 drum machine: 2.6 sextillion possible states, one thumb, and the argument (made in memes) that judgment is still the part that doesn’t compute.

The through line: the messy middle doesn’t get less messy because a model is helping. It just moves faster, and the wrong turns get easier to skip past.

Then everyone stayed and talked, which is the actual point.
