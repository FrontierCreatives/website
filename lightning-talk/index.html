Presenting at Frontier Creatives

Oh hello. You're seeing this page because we think you're special. You'd make a good presenter for our lightning talks. Want in? Here's the details.

The shape of it

Five to ten minutes, one story.

Tell a story. Make it engaging, and remember we're here to have fun and show the messy middle, not polished outcomes. Bring one project or one problem rather than a tour of everything you do.

The room spans Design, Founders, Engineering, and Product, but don't generalize for it. Go full nerd if you want. The specifics are what people take away. We're sharing how you think and operate for design inside the AI world.

If you need help putting a story together, or want input or direction, ask Ben. We want you to succeed, and we know what it takes to make something you're proud of. Let's keep this simple and easy for you.

Setup

Land these in the first minute.

  • Who are you?Your name, whether you're even a designer, and what you're working on.
  • What was your goal?Tell us what you were trying to achieve so we know how to read the rest of the talk. Give us the reasons you care so we can care too.
  • Name your stack.What tools did you use?

The messy middle

This is the bulk of the talk. It's also the part people write down.

  • Show us where it got hard, and how you got through it.Finished, polished work is a great subject, as long as the path to it stays visible. Think narrative storytelling.
  • Show us on screen.Real files, real prompts, the attempts that went nowhere. A recording or a diagram works too. We've all hit unexpected outputs and frustrations in agentic workflows, so watching you sidestep them is a real, and relatable, joy.
  • Show us the jigs.The helper tools, the file structure, the prompt, the script, the check you run before you trust the output.
  • What broke?Name it plainly. The failure's usually more useful than the win.

Learnings

Wrap it up and leave them something.

  • What did you learn?Name the outcome, or where it stands now if it's still moving. This is where the bigger point belongs, and where it works: we've just watched you do the thing, so tell us what you think it means.
  • Link out.A QR code if there's somewhere to send people.

What not to do

Short list, and we mean it kindly.

  • Don't show anything you're not free to show.No confidential IP, no NDA'd screens, no unreleased client work, no customer data or private names on screen. If you'd have to blur it, bring a different project. Check with your employer before you commit if there's any doubt; it's your call to make and your name on it.
  • Don't pitch your company.Nobody came for the deck you use with prospects. If your work is the product, show the work, not the positioning.
  • Don't tour your whole practice.One project, one problem. Breadth reads as a résumé; depth reads as a talk.
  • Don't show only the finished thing.A beautiful final artifact with no visible path to it is the one format that reliably loses this room.
  • Don't open with the conclusion.We asked the room what they came for and not one person asked for the state of AI in design; they asked to see how other people actually work. Show us that first. The big thought lands at the end, once we've watched you earn it.
  • Don't run long.Five to ten minutes. We'll be warm about it and we'll still cut you off.

Timeline

Our Volumes run once a month. We ask that you share a draft and check in with us about a week before the show. We can then give you a steer, or whatever help you need to get over the finish line.

Questions we get

  • Should I use AI for this?Yes. Your LLM probably already has memory of all these build conversations. Ask it to put the piece together first, then see what needs polishing.
  • Should I build a deck?Up to you, but we'd recommend it. Figma Slides, HTML, PowerPoint, or good old pinch and zoom all work, as long as you tell a story and have a basic narrative.
  • Can someone build this story with me?Yes. If you've got the pieces and a story outlined, Ben can lean in; he's gotten pretty good at this. You take it from there once there's a draft.
  • Is there anything I can use to start with?Yes. You can reference and download all the Frontier Creatives brand assets here.

That's the whole ask. One story, told honestly, in ten minutes or less.

Stuck, unsure, or want a second pair of eyes on an outline? Ask Ben. That offer's real and it isn't a formality.
